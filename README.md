# Amina Ali | Portfolio

A multidisciplinary portfolio featuring user experience design, user research, digital product development, and strategic communication.

## About

I study Informatics at the University of Washington, with interests in human-centered design, research, technology, and the ways digital experiences shape how people connect with information and one another. My work brings together thoughtful research, clear communication, and practical design decisions.

## Selected Work

- **Job Quest** — A gamified approach to supporting students through the job-search process, informed by user research and usability feedback.
- **CeraVe Skincare Assistant** — A beginner-friendly skincare experience built around personalized recommendations, clear guidance, and prototype testing.
- **charity: water** — A student-focused landing page exploring audience engagement, campaign messaging, and visual storytelling.
- **Course Registration Redesign** — A design exploration focused on making course planning and registration more accessible and understandable.
- **TravBuddy** — A travel companion discovery experience connecting people through shared destinations, dates, and interests.

Each project page highlights the challenge, approach, design decisions, and relevant research or evaluation work.
